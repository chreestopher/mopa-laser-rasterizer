Experimental Laboratories

Explore engraving tools that extend beyond the standard Rasterizer workflow. These laboratories are available for testing, but their interfaces, output, and requirements may continue to change as the experiments develop.

Holographic Etching Lab

Begin with a tested iridescent marking setting, generate and engrave a calibration grid, photograph the result, and analyze how hatch angle, line interval, and optional laser-setting variations change its appearance.

Keep useful measurements as a Holographic Recipe, then map artwork into varied diffraction-grating layers for an iridescent effect reminiscent of holograms. These marks are not true holograms.

Depthmap/Relief Engraving Lab

Load an image and initialize a relative depthmap locally with an in-browser depth model. Inspect the grayscale depth and relief projection, adjust the usable range, paint corrections, and optionally use a saved Depth Palette to let source colors influence interpreted depth.

Export an 8-bit or 16-bit grayscale depthmap for relief-engraving preparation. A single image cannot provide measured physical depth, so every result requires visual review and may need manual correction.
