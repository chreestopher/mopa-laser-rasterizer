Step 01 · Color Discovery Workflow

Choose a saved Swatch Palette or upload a LightBurn Material Library, sweep two laser parameters, and pack equal square cells edge-to-edge inside the requested dimensions.

Step 02 · Photograph and Measure

Choose a saved grid below, or manually enter the 8-character ID engraved on a grid or its full Grid ID. When you choose the finished-grid photograph, its saved metadata loads automatically and the page attempts to align the numbered overlay. Drag the four green corners to correct the cell-matrix boundary whenever needed. The photograph stays in this browser.

Choose a Grid ID and its photograph.

Step 03 · Review and Export

Click a measured swatch to enable or disable it. Export and save actions can include multiple enabled colors, with one swatch per official Rasterizer color. Refine Grid allows one swatch and creates a finer grid centered approximately on that setting.

The original sweep parameters retain their prior cell spacing. When you choose a different parameter, Color Lab starts with a conservative range around the selected swatch. Adjust any bound before generating the refinement grid. To sweep only one parameter, give the other parameter the same minimum and maximum value.
